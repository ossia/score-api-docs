◆ max_events

constexpr int Patternist::pattern_node::max_events = 4 * max_lanes * (2 * max_ratchet + 1)
staticconstexpr

Once half full, what is due goes out before the next grid point, so the queue holds at most the steps around the one playing: the one before (late offset and swing delay it by up to a step), this one and the next (scheduled early), with room to spare - each a note-on and a note-off per hit, and a release.

◆ origin

◆ release_reserve

constexpr int Patternist::pattern_node::release_reserve = 2 * max_lanes
