Nodes::Looper::audio_layer Struct Reference

Detailed Description

One pass of the loop: the first recording, or an overdub on top of it.

Its audio is kept apart from the other passes', so that undoing or erasing it is hiding it and its gain can decay with feedback without touching what it recorded.
