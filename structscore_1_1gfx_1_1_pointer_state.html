score::gfx::PointerState Struct Reference

Detailed Description

The pointers over an output: the mouse and the touch points, in the layout of vertexshaderart's touch history (greggman/vertexshaderart, src/js/main.js, addTouchPosition / addTouchPressure / getTouchIndex). Slot 0 is the mouse or the first finger, slot 1 the second finger, and so on, 32 of them.

Positions are in clip space: x and y in [-1, 1], y up (main.js x * 2 - 1, y * -2 + 1 over the canvas). A slot keeps its last position when released; only its pressure drops to 0, as on the site.

The window writes from the GUI thread, a renderer reads a snapshot from the render thread.
