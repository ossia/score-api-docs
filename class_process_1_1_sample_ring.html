Process::SampleRing Class Reference

Detailed Description

The most recent samples of a multichannel signal, written by the execution thread and read by the GUI without lock or allocation on the writing side.

The writer never waits: it overwrites the oldest samples. It announces the end of the block it is about to write before writing it, and publishes it once written; the reader copies the span it wants and checks afterwards that no block announced meanwhile reached it, as in a seqlock. A copy the writer may have overwritten is reported as unavailable rather than returned torn.

Only what was written as one continuous stream is returned: the frames of a channel from before its buffer existed or from before the writer skipped it, and the frames from before a pause of the writer (see restart()), are reported as unavailable.

Channel buffers are allocated by the reader (provision()) when the writer reports more channels than are allocated; until then the writer skips the extra channels. Buffers are freed only with the ring.
