const ossia::value &  v 
)
virtual

Take what the list supplies and leave the rest of the uniform alone.

ossia::convert<vec4f>(list) starts from a zeroed vector and fills the components the list has, so three floats arriving at a four-component uniform wrote a zero into the fourth. On a colour that is alpha, and the shape went black: an OSC address carrying rgb, or anything else that sends a colour without its alpha. A cable did not show it because it carries all four.

Leaving the missing components untouched is also the right answer for a non-colour uniform: a partial update should not silently zero what it did not mention.
