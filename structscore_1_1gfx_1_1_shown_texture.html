score::gfx::ShownTexture Struct Reference

Detailed Description

The 2D image the views show of a texture.

Single shows one slice, layer or face at the texture's size. Overview tiles up to max_tiles slices or layers, evenly spaced, row by row from the top left, and unfolds a cube as a cross: 

   +Y

-X +Z +X -Z -Y

The shaders of shownTextureGlsl() lay it out the same way.
