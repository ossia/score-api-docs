NvDvpResourceHandle score::gfx::interop::HostPinnedRing::Impl::dvpHandleFor ( QRhiTexture *  tex)
inlinenoexcept

Look up (or register-and-cache) the DVP handle for a destination texture. The cache key is the QRhiTexture pointer; assumes QRhi preserves texture identity across frames (which it does — the native handle may rebuild but the QRhiTexture* stays stable until the consumer calls destroy on it).
