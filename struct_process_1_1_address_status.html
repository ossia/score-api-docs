Detailed Description

Whether the cables of a port keep its address from taking effect.

The execution engine reads the address of an inlet only when it has no cable, or when the process at the other end of one of its immediate glutton cables is not playing. An outlet writes to its address under the same condition on its outgoing cables; a propagated audio outlet has a cable to its parent interval, which plays whenever the process does. Texture and geometry ports use their address next to their cables.
