Detailed Description

Plays a click on every beat of the bar.

Each click starts on the sample its beat falls on, inside the buffer, and plays at its own speed whatever the tempo. A click still sounding when the next one starts is faded out, so that fast tempos do not pile them up.

The sounds are handed over by the UI thread, already decoded and at the rate of the engine: the audio thread only reads them.
