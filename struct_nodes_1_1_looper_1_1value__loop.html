Detailed Description

The values of a loop, in passes, as a pass_stack whose material is the values and the stretches where each pass holds a key.

An impulse is fired where it was recorded, as a note: every pass is heard over the others. Any other value is held until the next one of its key: a pass holds a key from its first new value of it to its end, and, where it does, what the passes under it hold of that key is not heard. Before that first value, and after the pass, the passes under it are heard again.

The metadata of the values is in fixed storage; a value itself may own heap memory, see value_slot.
