Nodes::Looper::capture_window Struct Reference

Detailed Description

What the audio and the MIDI looper do the same way with the passes of their loop and with their transport: the tick, the buttons, undo, redo, erase, mute and the layers past Max layers, closing a take, Capture, the score stopping, the takes written and what the view is sent of them. The looper, Node, derives from looper_node<Node>, keeps its passes in a pass_stack it returns from stack(), and says what is its own:

A looper may also say that an overdub pass is a layer only once something comes in for it:

static constexpr bool passes_wait_for_material = true;

Such a pass starts open but out of the stack: it is not a layer, makes no room at Max layers, drops nothing redo would bring back, and the overdub goes on over it. The looper calls material_arriving() before it records the first thing of a pass, which makes the pass a layer starting where the overdub began, as far as it went; and pass_advanced() for what the pass goes over. A pass that never gets anything is no layer, no take, no merge. A looper that does not say so makes its passes layers as they start. What Capture takes over a loop: input frames [end - n; end[ of the capture clock, as the pass an overdub over them would have been. Frame end is at y_end in the coordinates the playhead goes forward in, which it goes over rate positions a frame; backwards, they are the loop read from its end.
