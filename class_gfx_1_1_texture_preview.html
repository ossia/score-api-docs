Detailed Description

What a texture outlet produces, as an image any view can paint: an inspector widget, an item of the scene.

When an output of the patch renders the producer, its render list reads the outlet back as it renders it (score::gfx::PreviewTap): the preview costs one small pass and readback, and shows what the output shows. Otherwise the preview has an offscreen output of its own, which pulls the producer: it then renders even when nothing else consumes it. The GfxContext switches between the two as outputs come and go; image() is the latest frame.
