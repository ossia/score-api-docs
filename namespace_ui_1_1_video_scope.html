Ui::VideoScope Namespace Reference

Detailed Description

Video scopes computed on the GPU: waveform, parades, vectorscope, histogram, CIE 1931 chromaticity and Tektronix-style diamond.

Three compute passes per frame: the bins of the previous frame fade by the persistence, every sampled input pixel adds to the bins of the mode, and the bins are tone-mapped into the scope image, which is the texture output. The labelled graticule is drawn on the CPU and composited on the GPU. Of a 3D texture, an array or a cube map, the scope analyses what the Layout shows: one slice, layer or face, or all of them side by side.

The view shows that output through a preview of the outlet: being an output of the graph, the preview pulls the scope, which renders whenever it is shown, cabled further or not.
