Detailed Description

The one V4L2 fourcc <-> VideoPixelFormat table.

Raw buffer layouts only. Compressed fourccs (MJPEG, JPEG, H.264, MPEG-4, CPIA) belong to the codec axis, not to a pixel-format vocabulary, and are resolved where the camera is enumerated.

There used to be two independent V4L2 tables – one to AVPixelFormat for camera enumeration, one to VideoPixelFormat for the DMA capture path – and they disagreed. Anything needing an AVPixelFormat now goes through VideoPixelFormatAV rather than maintaining a second table.
