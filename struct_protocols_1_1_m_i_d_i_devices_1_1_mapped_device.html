◆ level

bool Protocols::MIDIDevices::MappedDevice::level {true}

Build the controls under a level of their own, named after the description, rather than straight onto the root.

A port carrying one device does not need the level: it lengthens every address by a step named after whatever the document calls itself, which for an instrument patch can be a whole sentence. A port carrying several does, or their controls would collide.
