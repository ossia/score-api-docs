Detailed Description

The mouse on the bars of the sequence shown, in the layer and in the node.

As in the parameter bars of the pattern sequencer: a press sets the bar under the mouse and the drag every bar it goes over; Shift-drag moves every bar by the same amount; Alt-drag blends every bar towards a random value, the more the further the mouse goes.
