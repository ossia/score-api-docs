Detailed Description

template<class Lock>
struct score::gfx::interop::DvpOutputD3D11< Lock >

D3D11 output strategy via NVIDIA "GPUDirect for Video" (DVP).

D3D11 sibling of DvpOutputGl. Vendor-neutral: parameterized by the Lock page-lock policy + the encoder closures (makeEncoder / getOutputTexture). See DvpOutputGl for the full per-frame model.
