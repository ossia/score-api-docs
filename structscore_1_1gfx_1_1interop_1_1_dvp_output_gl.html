Detailed Description

template<class Lock>
struct score::gfx::interop::DvpOutputGl< Lock >

OpenGL output strategy via NVIDIA "GPUDirect for Video" (DVP).

Vendor-neutral: the per-vendor parts are the Lock page-lock policy and the two encoder closures — makeEncoder builds the fragment encoder for the card's wire format, getOutputTexture returns that encoder's output texture (the encoders expose m_outTexture as a public field, hence a per-format closure rather than a virtual). Per frame: encode RGBA->wire bytes into the encoder texture, DVP-DMA texture->page-locked sysmem, vendor ships sysmem.

The QRhi GL context must be current on the calling thread when init() runs and when transfer functions run.
