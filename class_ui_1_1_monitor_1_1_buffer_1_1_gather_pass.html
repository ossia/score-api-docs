Ui::Monitor::Buffer::GatherPass Class Reference

Detailed Description

Records the gather pass of a GatherPlan and the readback of its result.

The staging buffer grows to what the largest readback needed and is kept; so are the pipeline and the bindings while the source and the range it reads stay the same.
