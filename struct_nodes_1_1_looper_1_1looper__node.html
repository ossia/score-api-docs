A take starts once the last one is written, when a pass closed or the view asked for one. ◆ stop_score()

template<typename Node >
void Nodes::Looper::looper_node< Node >::stop_score ( )
protected

The score stops: what was being taken in is a pass like any other, and Mode goes from Record or Overdub to Play. The loop is kept: only the transport starts over. The looper is most often gone with the score's stop, and its views with nothing to ask of it: the take it leaves has the mix and every pass as they are, unless the last take has them already.
