Protocols::MIDIDevices::Scale Struct Reference

Detailed Description

What the numbers mean in the parameter's own terms, when that differs from what is sent: a knob sends 0-127 while the parameter reads -64..+63, or 0 to 100 %, or simply Left to Right.

Never changes what goes on the wire – Value::min and Value::max stay the only values a host may send. This is for what it shows.
