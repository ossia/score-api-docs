Detailed Description

A preview of what a node outputs, read back by a render list that already renders the node.

The tap hangs on the producer node (Node::previewTaps). A render list of an output of the patch which renders that node, can sample the port and keeps rendering (RenderList::previewHost) draws the port's texture, fitted into size, into a small target of its own after the nodes have rendered, and reads it back into readback. Nothing of the chain is rendered again: the preview sees the frame the output renders.

A node no output of the patch renders is rendered for the previews by their shared output, PreviewHubNode, whose list reads the taps back the same way. See GfxContext::add_preview.

◆ readable

bool score::gfx::PreviewTap::readable {true}

False while the hub, hosting the tap, cannot read the port (no texture it could sample): the preview then renders it on an output of its own.

◆ wanted

std::atomic_bool score::gfx::PreviewTap::wanted {true}

Set when the consumer has taken the latest image: the next frame of the hosting list is read back, the ones after it are not until the consumer asks again. A readback costs a pass and, on OpenGL, a synchronous glReadPixels: the views repaint far less often than the outputs render.
