Detailed Description

Draws every primitive of a scene with its world transform, in the shading modes of the Geometry viewer, and the lights and cameras of the scene as line gizmos. Part of the viewer's renderer, on the render thread.

The scene is walked when it changes, not every frame. A mesh is uploaded once, and kept for as long as the buffers of its primitive stay the same: a scene whose transforms only move does not upload anything again.
