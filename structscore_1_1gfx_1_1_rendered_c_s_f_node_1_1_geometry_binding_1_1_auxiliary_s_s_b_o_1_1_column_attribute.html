Detailed Description

The upstream layout the table was resolved against (its attributes; its inputs' buffer and offset, then its bindings' strides; its buffers), and the buffers it left bound: an unchanged upstream is not resolved again. Compared field by field, as upstream nodes may hand over a new geometry object every frame for the same layout.
