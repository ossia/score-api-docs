Detailed Description

A modifier drag over a row of bars, each the value of a step.

Offset (Shift) moves every bar by the same amount, as far as the mouse went up or down. Random (Alt) blends each bar towards a random target, the more the further the mouse goes from where it pressed, whatever the direction. Either acts on steps only; the other bars keep their values.

bottom is the value at the bottom of the bars and top the one at the top: a row drawn upside down passes them the other way round.
