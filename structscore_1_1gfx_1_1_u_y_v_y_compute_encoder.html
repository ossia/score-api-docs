Detailed Description

Compute-shader RGBA -> UYVY (2vuy) encoder targeting an external SSBO.

UYVY packs 2 source pixels into 1 little-endian 32-bit word (Cb, Y0, Cr, Y1) at 8-bit-per-component precision. Each compute thread handles one pixel pair. Designed for the AJA tier-3 path: writes go directly to a CUDA-importable, AJA-DMA-locked GPU buffer.
