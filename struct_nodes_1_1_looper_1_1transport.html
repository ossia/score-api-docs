◆ ask()

void Nodes::Looper::transport::ask ( LoopMode  m,
bool  empty 
)
inlinenoexcept

Mode set to m, from its port or by Play, Stop, Record or Overdub: what is asked for from now on, whatever was waiting for the grid. Overdubbing an empty loop records it, as Record does, and the recording then gives way to overdubbing. Recording over a loop overdubs it, as on a looper pedal: a new loop is recorded once the loop is cleared. empty: there is no loop and none is being recorded.

An action. Rec / Play / Dub and Play / Stop act on the mode the looper will be in once what waits for the grid is taken up: pressed again before the grid point, they queue their change after it, and each change is taken up on a grid point of its own. empty: there is no loop and none is being recorded.
