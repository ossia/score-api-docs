Ui::Monitor::Color Namespace Reference

Detailed Description

What the image scopes' graticules are drawn from: luma and colour difference per ITU-R BT.601, BT.709 and BT.2020, bar targets, the PQ signal of a luminance, primaries and the CIE 1931 loci. The per-pixel transfer functions are the GPU ones of Gfx/Graph/decoders/ColorSpace.hpp.
