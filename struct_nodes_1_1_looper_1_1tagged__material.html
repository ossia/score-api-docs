Detailed Description

template<int MaxPasses>
struct Nodes::Looper::tagged_material< MaxPasses >

Material kept apart from the passes, each piece tagged with pass, the index of its pass, replaced_by, the passes that replace it, a bit each, and merged, its merge_marks: how a pass_stack's hooks move it along.

Two indices past every pass there can be hold the material of the layer set aside, and that of a muted pass a merge lost, which undoing the merge gives back.
